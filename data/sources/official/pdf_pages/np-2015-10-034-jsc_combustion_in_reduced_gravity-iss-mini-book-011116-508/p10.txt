Benefits of conducting combustion research in microgravity can thus be
summarized as:
• Removal of the complex interaction between the buoyant flow and the energy
feedback to the flame.
• Realistic simulation of the fire risk seen in manned spacecraft.
• Practical simulation of the gravitational environment seen for reacting systems
in future spacecraft.
