OH* Imaging
•

OH* is an excited (radical) molecule that emits light at 310 nm, which is in the ultraviolet, when
it drops to a lower energy state in a process known as chemiluminescence.

•

ACME will carry out OH* imaging using CIR’s Low-Light-Level Ultraviolet (LLL-UV) package, which
is a monochrome camera with an intensiﬁer (for 280-700 nm) and a 310-nm bandpass ﬁlter
(with a 10-nm FWHM) for OH* imaging.
