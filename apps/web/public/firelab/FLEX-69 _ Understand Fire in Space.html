The big idea

On Earth, hot gases rise. In space, that upward flow is greatly reduced.

On Earth, gravity drives buoyant flow: warm combustion gases rise and fresh air moves in. In the ISS microgravity environment, buoyant flow is greatly reduced, so diffusion and imposed airflow become especially important.

Why should we care? Spacecraft fire-safety designs need evidence about ignition, flame behavior and extinction under microgravity conditions—not only Earth-based intuition.
